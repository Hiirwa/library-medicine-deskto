LIBRARY MEDICINE DESKTOP - BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Please read these notes. Choose "I accept" to continue.

WHAT THIS APP DOES
Library Medicine Desktop lets you collect and edit research records without internet. Your changes are saved on this computer and are sent to librarymedicine.online automatically when you are back online.

WHAT IS STORED ON THIS COMPUTER
- A local database with the projects, forms, variables and records your account can access.
- Attachments, in Documents\Library Medicine.
- A personal access token, kept in the Windows Credential Manager and encrypted with your Windows account. Your password is never stored.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES
- Use the app only with an account you are authorised to use, and only for research data you are allowed to handle.
- Keep this computer secure: use a Windows password, keep Windows up to date and lock the screen when you leave it. Research records can contain personal data.
- Signing out removes the offline copy of your data from this computer unless you choose to keep it.

UNINSTALLING
Uninstalling removes the program. You can also delete your offline data during uninstall. Data already synced to librarymedicine.online is not affected.
